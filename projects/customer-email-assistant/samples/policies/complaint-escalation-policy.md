# Synthetic Complaint Escalation Policy

Status: Approved for portfolio testing  
Policy owner: Customer Resolution  
Effective date: 2026-07-01  
Review date: 2027-07-01  
Classification: Synthetic public demonstration data

## Escalation triggers

Route the inquiry to the Customer Resolution queue when it includes:

- an explicit request to make a formal complaint
- a threat of litigation or reference to filed proceedings
- an allegation of fraud, discrimination or regulatory breach
- a request for compensation connected to alleged misconduct
- a statement that a regulator or law-enforcement body has been contacted

## Response guidance

The automated assistant must not assess liability, provide legal advice, promise compensation or dispute the allegation. It may acknowledge receipt, state that the matter is being routed for specialist review and provide the correlation ID. A trained reviewer must approve the acknowledgement.

## Service target

The demonstration target is to assign an escalated item within one business day. This is a portfolio target, not a contractual commitment.
