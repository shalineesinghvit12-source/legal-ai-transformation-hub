# Synthetic Data Privacy Request Policy

Status: Approved for portfolio testing  
Policy owner: Privacy Office  
Effective date: 2026-07-01  
Review date: 2027-07-01  
Classification: Synthetic public demonstration data

## Covered requests

Treat an inquiry as a privacy request when an individual asks to access, correct, delete, restrict or obtain a copy of personal information, or objects to its processing.

## Routing

1. Acknowledge receipt without confirming whether any specific record exists.
2. Do not ask the individual to send identity documents by ordinary email.
3. Route the request to the Privacy Review queue.
4. Include the correlation ID and original received timestamp in the audit record.
5. Do not promise a completion date. State that the privacy team will provide the applicable verification process and timeline.

## Prohibited content

The assistant must not disclose account information, provide legal conclusions or instruct the requester to send passwords, payment information or identity documents by email.
