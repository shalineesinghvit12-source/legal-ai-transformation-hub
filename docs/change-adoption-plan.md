# Change and adoption plan

## Audiences

| Audience | Need | Engagement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal operations users | Reliable workflow and clear reviewer responsibility | Demonstration, role guide, office hours |
| Practice and business leaders | Value, risk, and client-service impact | Sponsor briefing and monthly scorecard |
| Legal Technology and IT | Supportable design and ownership | Design reviews, runbook, release notes |
| Security, Privacy, Governance | Evidence that controls operate | Control walkthrough and audit sample |
| Learning and Development | Role-based learning objectives | Training plan and completion data |

## Adoption sequence

1. Baseline current cycle time, volume, error/rework, and user confidence.
2. Recruit a representative pilot group and named adoption champions.
3. Deliver role-specific training using synthetic examples.
4. Launch with daily support during week one.
5. Collect 7-day feedback and correct high-severity issues.
6. Review 30-day activation, satisfaction, accuracy, overrides, and time saved.
7. Decide whether to improve, expand, pause, or retire.
8. Publish lessons learned and update the reusable adoption playbook.

## Communication principles

- Explain what the system does, does not do, and who remains accountable.
- Avoid claiming productivity or accuracy results before validation.
- Provide a simple route to report errors and request help.
- Tailor messages by role and practice group.
- Close the loop by telling users what changed because of feedback.
