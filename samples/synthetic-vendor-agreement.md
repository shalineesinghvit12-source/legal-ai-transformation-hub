# SYNTHETIC VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Demonstration data only. This is not a valid legal agreement.**

This Vendor Services Agreement is made effective on March 1, 2026 between Northwind Legal Operations LLC, an Illinois limited liability company ("Customer"), and Contoso Document Services Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Vendor").

## Services

Vendor will provide document-indexing and administrative support services described in mutually approved statements of work. Vendor may not use Customer data for advertising, model training, or purposes unrelated to the services.

## Term and renewal

The initial term begins March 1, 2026 and ends February 28, 2027. The agreement renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

## Fees and payment

Customer will pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt. Total fees under the initial term will not exceed $120,000 without a signed change order.

## Confidentiality and security

Vendor must protect confidential information using reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. Vendor must notify Customer of a confirmed security incident without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after confirmation.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days after written notice. Customer may terminate for convenience with 45 days written notice.

## Data return

Within 15 days after termination, Vendor will return or securely delete Customer data, except where retention is required by law. Vendor will provide written certification of deletion upon request.

## Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

## Signatures

The parties acknowledge that this synthetic document is solely for a technology demonstration and creates no legal rights or obligations.
