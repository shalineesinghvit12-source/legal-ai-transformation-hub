# KPI catalog

| KPI | Definition | Source | Cadence | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opportunity conversion | Approved or conditional opportunities / submitted opportunities | Opportunity | Monthly | Transformation Manager |
| Prioritization lead time | Median days from Submitted to priority decision | Opportunity, AuditEvent | Weekly | PMO |
| Governance lead time | Median days from Governance entry to final decision | GovernanceAssessment, AuditEvent | Weekly | Governance Lead |
| Pilot cycle time | Median days from approved pilot to acceptance decision | Opportunity, AuditEvent | Monthly | Product Owner |
| Automation success | Successful document runs / total eligible runs | DocumentReview | Daily | Platform Team |
| Field accuracy | Correct extracted fields / evaluated fields | Evaluation dataset | Release and monthly | QA Lead |
| Human override rate | Reviewed fields changed by human / reviewed fields | AuditEvent | Weekly | Product Owner |
| Training completion | Trained users / invited target users | AdoptionEvent | Weekly | L&D |
| 30-day activation | Users active during first 30 days / trained users | AdoptionEvent | Monthly | Product Owner |
| Satisfaction | Mean 1-5 satisfaction among valid responses | Feedback | Monthly | Product Owner |
| User confidence | Mean 1-5 confidence among valid responses | Feedback | Monthly | Product Owner |
| Validated hours saved | Approved time savings based on measured baseline and pilot sample | Opportunity | Quarterly | Business Sponsor |
| Benefit realization | Validated benefit / approved expected benefit | Opportunity | Quarterly | Business Sponsor |
| Open critical risks | Critical unresolved risk records | GovernanceAssessment or RAID | Weekly | Transformation Manager |

## Reporting rules

- Separate expected, estimated, and validated benefits.
- Display sample values as synthetic in the portfolio demonstration.
- Show metric owner, refresh time, reporting period, and filters.
- Do not average percentages across unequal populations without weighting.
- Reconcile dashboard totals with source tables before leadership distribution.
- Suppress or aggregate small populations when privacy policy requires it.
